Why VAMS?

Eliminate Security Loopholes and Paper Delays

Traditional security logbooks at building gates rely on unverified manual handwriting, fake phone numbers, unmonitored exits, and zero facility headcount visibility. VAMS completely transforms physical entry into an automated, transparent, and auditable digital workflow.

The Old Way: Visitors scribble in paper registers. Guards guess permissions. Zero knowledge of who is inside during emergencies.
The VAMS Solution: Email-verified user registration, manager authorization, cryptographically unique QR passes, and live gate-level logging.
Instant Benefits
  • Touchless & Mobile: Scan with any smartphone camera or cheap tablet.
  • Real-time Security: Revoke or expire passes with a single click.
  • Zero Hardware Investment: No proprietary RFID readers or smartcards.
  • Complete Accountability: Every approval, gate check, and entry timestamp recorded forever.
System Capabilities

Built for Real-World Security Operations

Everything needed to manage visitors, guards, gates, and approvals under one unified platform.

Dynamic QR Passes

Unique cryptographic tokens generated automatically upon manager approval. Rendered instantly on user dashboard with full status indicators.

High-Speed Scanner

HTML5 camera integration works seamlessly on Android, iOS, tablets, and laptops. Sub-second decoding with large visual PASS/FAIL cards.

Multi-Gate Policy Lock

Configure individual gates with granular permissions: Public, Employee Only, VIP Restricted, or All Access. Guard stations strictly enforce rules.

Employees select their stationed gate; Managers confirm deployment. Scanner locks automatically to that gate to prevent erroneous gate scans.

Instant calculation of active occupants inside the facility (Total Entries - Total Exits). Vital for safety and capacity limits.

Dual Immutable Logs

Distinct audit records for Administrative Changes (SystemLog) and Gate Movements (ScanLog) that cannot be altered.

How It Works

End-to-End Access Journey

From initial registration to physical gate scan in four streamlined steps.

1
Register & Verify

Users sign up as Guest or Employee and select their requested gate. A secure Firebase verification link ensures verified email authenticity.

2
Manager Review

Managers review pending requests in their dashboard, setting access tier (Public, VIP) and validity (Single / Ongoing) before approving.

3
Guard Stationing

Guards choose their designated gate and request assignment. Once approved, the scanner automatically locks to that gate.

4
Fast QR Scan

At the gate, the visitor presents their QR pass to the guard's camera. The system checks validity, gate permission, and logs entry/exit.

Security & Compliance

Enterprise-Grade Audit Integrity

Accountability is the backbone of facility security. VAMS ensures every single user state change and physical movement is recorded in an immutable historical ledger.

Immutable System Logs

Tracks all administrative events: account creation, approvals, rejections, and access revocations with actor timestamps.

Gate Scan Logs

Records every physical badge-in and badge-out event, associating user ID, gate location, timestamp, and ENTRY/EXIT status.

Firebase Email Authentication

Eliminates dummy or bot signups by restricting dashboard access until email verification tokens are confirmed.
